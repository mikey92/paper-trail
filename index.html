Paper Trail

Print a field card for your walk: the species people have seen there at this time of year, each with one line on how to recognise it. Gemma writes the lines here, in your browser.

About

The species come from research-grade iNaturalist observations near the place, in the same month of past years. Each line starts from the sentences in the species' Wikipedia description. Gemma 3 rewrites them on your device, and a line that uses a word, number or colour those sentences don't back up is tried once more, then replaced by a quote.

There is no server. Your last choices stay in this browser. A location from your device is rounded to about a kilometre before it goes to iNaturalist and Open-Meteo, and the model never sees it. Source and evaluation on GitHub (MIT).
